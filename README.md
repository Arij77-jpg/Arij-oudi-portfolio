# Arij Oudi — Personal Portfolio

A modern, responsive developer portfolio built with pure **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript**, tailored from Arij Oudi's curriculum vitae.

## Features
- **Sleek Dark Tech / AI & DevOps Aesthetic**: Persistent **Dark / Light mode toggle** with custom CSS variables and smooth transitions.
- **Interactive Hero & Identity**:
  - Real portrait photo identity card with active status badge.
  - Interactive constellation particle canvas with cursor magnetism.
  - Dynamic typewriter animation displaying developer roles.
  - **Quick CV Access**: Dedicated **View CV** (browser preview) and **Download CV (PDF)** action buttons.
- **Header & Navigation**:
  - Smooth in-page anchor navigation.
  - Instant **CV** access badge in navigation actions bar.
  - Theme toggle (Dark / Light) with local storage persistence.
- **Complete CV & Academic Showcase**:
  - **About & Education**: Horizon School of Digital Technology, Double Degree Program with **Beijing Polytechnic**, Double Diploma Program with **ESTIAM Paris**, and High School Diploma (2024).
  - **Interactive CV Card**: Dedicated card with one-click PDF viewing and direct download.
  - **Projects Showcase**: Filterable portfolio (*All*, *Mobile & CivicTech*, *Full-Stack Systems*, *Interactive & Algorithms*, *Robotics & IoT*) with deep-dive detail modals.
    - *Bledi+ (بلادي+)*: CivicTech mobile platform (React Native, Expo, TypeScript, Firebase).
    - *HorizonExam*: Multi-role examination management platform.
    - *The Snitch's Path* & *Adventure Time Treasure Hunt*: Algorithmic and 2D games.
    - *Robotics & Embedded*: Robot Téléguidé, Smart Home & IoT, Ski Téléguidé, Smart City, Robot Tout-Terrain.
  - **Technical Skills**: Mobile, Web & Backend, Databases & Cloud (Firebase, PostgreSQL, etc.), Tools & Methodologies (Docker, Git, Agile, TDD), and Spoken Languages.
  - **Certifications & Specialized Trainings**: NVIDIA (Deep Learning), Meta (Front-End), HP LIFE, 15+ Robotics Certifications, FTSAAA Aeronautics/Cerf-volant, Graphic Design, and Secourisme (First Aid).
  - **Leadership, Activities & 4-Album Photo Gallery**:
    - 53 real event photos categorized into 6 interactive albums (Robotics Competitions, Leadership & GAICA, Hardware Builds, Teaching & Workshops, ISWIT Tunisia, and Chinese Double Degree Beijing Polytechnic) with full-screen lightbox.
    - Leadership entries for HoriBot Club, GDSC, SFE, IEEE, Scouts, ISWIT Organizing Committee (2 Years), Collège Mouansa, École de la Vie, GAICA Global AI Congress, and national robotics competitions.
  - **Contact Section**: One-click email copy, direct links, CV download link, and prefilled email composer.

